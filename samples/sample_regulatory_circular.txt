Circular No: DEMO/REG/2026/014
Subject: Guidance on Claim Settlement Timelines and Grievance Redressal

Clause 1. Applicability
1.1 This circular applies to all insurers offering health and general insurance products.

Clause 2. Claim Settlement Timelines
2.1 Insurers shall communicate a decision on cashless pre-authorization requests within 1 hour
of receiving complete information from the network provider.
2.2 Insurers shall settle or reject reimbursement claims within 15 days of receipt of all
necessary documents from the policyholder.
2.3 Any delay beyond the timelines in 2.1 and 2.2 shall attract interest at the rate specified
by the Authority, payable to the policyholder.

Clause 3. Grievance Redressal
3.1 Every insurer shall maintain a grievance redressal cell with a defined escalation matrix.
3.2 Unresolved grievances after 15 days may be escalated to the Insurance Ombudsman.

Clause 4. KYC Requirements for High-Value Claims
4.1 For claims exceeding the threshold specified in Annexure B, insurers shall re-verify the
identity of the claimant using at least one government-issued identity document.

Clause 5. Amendment History
5.1 This circular supersedes DEMO/REG/2024/009 with effect from the date of issue.
