SAMPLE HEALTH INSURANCE POLICY WORDING
UIN: DEMO-HLTH-001 (synthetic, for testing only)
Insurer: Demo General Insurance Ltd.

Section 1. Definitions
1.1 "Hospitalization" means admission to a hospital for a minimum continuous period of 24 hours.
1.2 "Sum Insured" means the amount stated in the policy schedule against which cover is provided.
1.3 "Waiting Period" means the period during which specified illnesses or conditions are not covered.

Section 2. Coverage
2.1 The Company will indemnify Medical Expenses incurred by the Insured Person during Hospitalization,
up to the Sum Insured, subject to the terms, conditions and exclusions of this Policy.
2.2 Pre-hospitalization expenses incurred up to 30 days prior to Hospitalization are covered.
2.3 Post-hospitalization expenses incurred up to 60 days after discharge are covered.

Section 3. Waiting Periods
3.1 A waiting period of 30 days applies from the policy start date for all illnesses, except accidents.
3.2 A waiting period of 24 months applies to specified conditions listed in Annexure A.
3.3 Pre-existing diseases are covered only after a continuous waiting period of 36 months.

Section 4. Portability
4.1 The Insured Person may port this policy to another insurer by applying at least 45 days before,
but not earlier than 60 days from, the policy renewal date.
4.2 On portability, accrued continuity benefits such as waiver of waiting periods already served
shall be carried forward, subject to the sum insured under the previous policy.

Section 5. Claims
5.1 Cashless claims require pre-authorization from the Company's network hospital desk.
5.2 Reimbursement claims must be filed within 30 days of discharge from hospital.
5.3 The Company shall settle or reject a claim within 15 days of receipt of all required documents.

Section 6. Exclusions
6.1 Cosmetic or plastic surgery, unless necessitated by an accident, is excluded.
6.2 Treatment for obesity or weight control is excluded unless medically necessary and pre-approved.
6.3 Self-inflicted injury or injury under the influence of intoxicants is excluded.

Section 7. Co-payment
7.1 A co-payment of 10% applies to all claims where treatment is taken outside the Company's
preferred provider network.
